Baking Blog: Best Chocolate Chip Cookies - Recipe

Best Chocolate Chip Cookies

(original recipe submitted by Dora on allrecipes.com)

Soft, chewy cookies with crispy edges, filled with delicious chocolate chips! This is the best recipe for chocolate chip cookies!.. (Just be careful not to overmix it...)

Personal rating: (Print by double clicking and selecting the "print" option)

Recipe yields 4 dozen cookies (48 servings)
1 serving = 1 cookie
cook time: 10 mins
prep time: 20 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter (softened)
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar (packed)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
    (I like to add 1 tsp of
    almond extract as well,
    it really compliments
    the vanilla - I let mine
    sit together in a bowl to
    meld the flavors for a few
    mins first)
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tsp hot water
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup chopped walnuts (optional)


Directions

Step 1

First, preheat your oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit. Now gather your ingredients. Then combine your sugars and butter in a bowl until your mixture is creamy and smooth.

Step 2

Next, beat in the eggs and add vanilla. (If you're using almond extract as well, I put mine in with the vanilla.)

Step 3

Now add the baking soda once it's dissolved in the hot water, and also add your salt. Mix until combined.

Step 4

Slowly add flour and mix until a soft dough forms, then add in your chocolate chips (and walnuts).

Step 5

Roll bits of the cookie dough into small balls, and place them on baking sheets or parchment paper.

Step 6

Bake in preheated oven until the cookies' edges are a light brown, about 10 minutes.<--I bake them anywhere between 10-12 mins

Step 7

Once your cookies have cooled enough for you to move them, place them on a wire rack to cool completely. Then enjoy!
